❓ Questions

Is Rupee Radius really free?

Yes. Every lesson is free to watch and share. We don't sell financial products.

Is this financial advice?

No. Our lessons are general education. Examples use illustrative numbers; please talk to a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

Who are the lessons for?

Everyone: students, first-jobbers, parents and grandparents. If you earn, spend or save money, these basics are for you.

Which language do you use?

Hinglish: simple English with everyday Hindi phrases, plus clear on-screen visuals so you can follow even on mute.
